# English Literature

The **literature** strand of the 5B INT English syllabus, studied in English.
Three blocks, two of which run as **interdisciplinary paths** alongside Italian
literature: the **War Poets** of the First World War (next to Ungaretti) and
**James Joyce** (next to the psychological novel of Svevo). The third is
**George Orwell**'s *1984* and dystopian fiction.

## Sections

- [**War Poets**](01-war-poets/index.md) — the soldier-poets of the Great War:
  Rupert Brooke, Wilfred Owen and Siegfried Sassoon, from patriotism to protest.
- [**George Orwell — *1984***](02-george-orwell/index.md) — what dystopian
  fiction is, and the world of Big Brother, Newspeak and the Party.
- [**James Joyce — *Dubliners***](03-james-joyce/index.md) — paralysis,
  epiphany and the short story *Eveline*.
